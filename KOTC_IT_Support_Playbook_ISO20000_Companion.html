IT Support Playbook
ISO/IEC 20000 + ITSM visual operating model

This companion adds a visual control layer to the existing playbook: service management governance, value-stream thinking, measurable controls, and continual improvement. It is an implementation aid—not a statement of certification or conformity.

ISO/IEC 20000-1 aligned conceptsITIL practice languageAccessible inline SVGResponsive

1. Governance & SMS

Define the service management system, scope, policies, authorities, interested parties, and documented information.

  • Named service owner and process owners
  • Policy, objectives, risk and compliance register
  • Document control, review and approval cadence

2. Service value stream

Connect demand to value through a consistent, auditable path from request or incident to resolution and improvement.

Demand→Record→Prioritise→Resolve→Learn

3. Incident & major incident

Restore agreed service quickly while protecting evidence, communications, and user confidence.

  • Impact × urgency priority matrix
  • Major incident commander, bridge, timeline and RCA
  • SLA clock, escalation and closure validation

4. Problem, knowledge & CSI

Reduce recurrence by identifying trends, recording known errors, publishing reusable knowledge, and tracking improvement actions.

  • Trend trigger and problem candidate criteria
  • Root cause, workaround, known error and permanent fix
  • CSI register with owner, benefit, measure and review date

5. Request, change & release

Separate standard fulfilment from controlled change. Assess risk, authorise, test, implement, verify and close with evidence.

RFC→Assess→Approve→Implement→Review

6. Asset, configuration & suppliers

Maintain trustworthy configuration information and supplier controls so decisions are based on known relationships, ownership and risk.

  • CI ownership, lifecycle and verification
  • Service-to-CI relationship map
  • Supplier SLA, risk, performance and review records
